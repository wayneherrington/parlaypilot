ParlayPilot — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Applies to the ParlayPilot web app and Android app

The short version: ParlayPilot has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. We run no server that receives your information. The tickets you type into the app are stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage, and are never sent to us or to anyone else. We do not sell personal data — we never receive any in the first place.

1. What the app stores, and where

  • Tickets you add. When you use "Add a ticket," the ticket name, platform, stake, payout, and legs you type are saved in your browser's local storage on your device (storage key parlaypilot-tickets-v1). This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer, and ParlayPilot has no backend server to transmit it to.
  • App files for fast, offline loading. The app's service worker caches the app's own files (the page, its manifest, and its icons) on your device so the app loads quickly and works offline. This cache contains no personal information.
  • Built-in demo content. The app ships with a built-in archive of settled demonstration tickets. That content is part of the app download itself and involves no data about you.

2. What we collect

Nothing. ParlayPilot contains no analytics software, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests to send data anywhere. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, betting history, or usage behavior, because the app never sends them anywhere.

3. Hosting and platform providers

The app's files are served as a static website by GitHub Pages, and the Android app opens that website in your browser engine (a Trusted Web Activity). Like any web host, GitHub may process standard technical information (such as IP addresses) in server logs when your device downloads the app's files; that processing is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement. If you downloaded the app from Google Play, Google's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Neither provider receives the tickets you store in the app — those never leave your device.

4. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal data to third parties, for the simple reason that we do not collect or receive any.

5. Deleting your data

Because your tickets live only on your device, you are in full control of them. To erase them, clear the site data for ParlayPilot in your browser or app settings, or uninstall the app — uninstalling removes the app's stored data from the device. There is nothing stored on our side to delete, because there is no "our side."

6. Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Data you enter never travels over the network at all, which is the strongest protection we can offer: it cannot be intercepted, leaked, or breached from a server we do not operate.

7. Who the app is for

ParlayPilot is a bet-tracking tool intended for adults of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction (21+ in most U.S. states). It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. ParlayPilot is not a sportsbook: it does not accept, place, or settle wagers and never holds user funds.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of ParlayPilot adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account), this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the update will be posted at this same address before the feature launches.
